WORQ

Closing the communication gap between technicians and facility managers — what changed, what's proven, and what's next.

DO NOT PROCEED

The original process required every single work request — no matter how small — to stop and wait for a live Facility Manager consultation before anything could happen.

"All WORQ requests must be reviewed with the Facility Manager prior to authorization or execution."

What Changed

Every request, no exceptions
→
Only the requests that need a decision
A live conversation, every time
→
One guided form, filled in once
A description, and nothing else
→
Photos, captioned by the tech
Routine jobs at or under $0 go straight through.

The Tool — Techs

  • 1 Same fields FMS already uses — one guided form instead of a live call.
  • 2 Hides what doesn't apply — no asset field needed for a third-party vendor call.
  • 3 Flags anything over $499 automatically for FM review.
  • 4 Produces the exact WORQ email — copy it, or open it directly in the mail app.

Photos, Captioned by the Tech

  • 1 The FM sees the actual problem — not just a written description.
  • 2 Each photo can be downloaded with its caption burned right into the image.
  • 3 Self-explanatory even once it's just an attachment in an email or work order.

The Tool — Facility Managers

→

Approve, deny, or forward — one click creates the Corrigo work order for the clear cases. Anything with a vendor or a capital call still comes to the FM.

What's Next — Honestly

1

Corrigo API access

Real one-click work order creation needs OAuth credentials from the Corrigo admin. Until then, it's a proposal — the intake tool is live today.

2

Corrigo's own alerts

Approve/deny/forward outcomes should notify the tech through Corrigo's existing Alerts and CruChats — one place to check, not a new system.

3

Smartsheet option

The company already uses it. Its Forms, Approvals, and Dashboards could cover a real chunk of this natively — worth a conversation.

Bottom line

The accountability the original process protected stays intact.
What disappears is the waiting.

  • FM still has final say on funding, vendor, and classification
  • Nothing spent past the approved amount without sign-off
  • Every job still tracked and classified before close-out
